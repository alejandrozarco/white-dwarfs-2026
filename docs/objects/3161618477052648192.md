# WDJ072758.87+101157.08 (Gaia DR3 3161618477052648192)

## Photometric periods

DC white dwarf (GF21 H-atmosphere 7.6 kK, 0.77 Msun), G = 17.40, 59 pc; the period was found in ZTF and recovered in three TESS sectors and in ATLAS; the highest TESS peak (7.52 c/d) belongs to another star in the aperture. Steen et al. (2024) list it as a likely spotted variable at P = 0.4914 h, the one-cycle-per-day alias of this period.

**Measurement.** P = 0.48155 ± 0.0 h (ZTF, n = 1106, BJD 2458205.655-2460949.003); semi-amplitude 2.5% ± 0.1%.
