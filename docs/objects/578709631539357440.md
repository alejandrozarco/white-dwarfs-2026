# WD 0856+048 (Gaia DR3 578709631539357440)

## Ca II triplet emission (gaseous discs)

White dwarf, G = 18.341; catalogued: SnowWhite DA; SIMBAD WD*/DA; MWDD DA; DESI DR1 DA.

**Measurement.** Ca II triplet emission (double-peaked, peak velocities -310/360 km/s); EW 17.55 ± 0.45 A in the SDSS-V coadd; other spectra: SDSS 2003-01-31, BOSS 2010-12-05, DESI 2022-03-07, X-shooter 2025-04-23 (2 spectra); gas_disc_epochs_578709631539357440.csv.

Equivalent width in the 10 dated spectra with EW > 3 times its error: 10.52-32.86 A; $`\chi^2`$ = 120.6 for 9 degrees of freedom with formal and line-free errors combined (gas_disc_variability.csv).

Scale of the SDSS-V coadd profile: BOSS-DR17 2010-12-05 0.016 ± 0.093 (24 continuum choices: -0.13 to 0.26); SDSS-DR17 2003-01-31 0.626 ± 0.257 (24 continuum choices: -0.51 to 0.78).

X-shooter UVB: Mg II 4481 absorption EW 0.131 ± 0.025 A and 0.227 ± 0.014 A; Ca II K absorption Gaussian depth 0.151 ± 0.023 and 0.17 ± 0.016.

Legacy Surveys DR10 W1 19.1 ± 1.8 uJy, W2 11.1 ± 4.2 uJy; Montreal DA 18181 K, log g 8.15 model 8.3 and 4.5 uJy (gas_disc_infrared.csv).
