# Zeeman splitting

`tables/magnetic_zeeman.csv` lists 32 SDSS-V white dwarfs whose H-alpha and H-beta are split into Zeeman components. Each line is fitted with three Gaussian components. For one star (Gaia DR3 2102849596247412480) the H-alpha fit is unconstrained in the coadd and only H-beta values are given. B_split is the linear-Zeeman field implied by the separation of the two outer components (method in [METHODS.md](../METHODS.md#methods)).

The overview shows H-alpha of the six spectra with the highest S/N; red lines mark the fitted component centres.

Consistency between the two lines: for 28 of the 31 stars with both lines fitted the H-alpha and H-beta fields agree to within 1 MG. For three (Gaia DR3 375892788968158848, 5665371272869153152 and 5848754492268362624) the H-beta fit gives a field 1.2 to 5 MG below the H-alpha value, with the H-beta sigma components far closer to the central component than the H-alpha field requires; the field of these three is not established and they are marked as inconsistent in the index. The formal errors in the table come from the line-centre fits alone: 10 of the 31 stars with both lines fitted differ between the two lines by more than three formal sigma, so the real uncertainty of a single-line field is a few tenths of a MG, not the tabulated hundredths.

The 31st star in the table (Gaia DR3 2102849596247412480) is H-beta only: its H-alpha fit is unconstrained, and it is neither counted as consistent nor as inconsistent.

Gaia DR3 5863057420550886144 has no Astra mwmVisit spectrum; its row (`spectrum` = BOSS coadd) is fitted on the coadd of three BOSS v6_2_1 spec-lite epochs (listed in `data/boss_epochs.csv`) used as delivered.

Context: GALEX J231613.4-552927 (Gaia DR3 6499095244738784128) is listed by Rebassa-Mansergas et al. (2019) as an infrared-excess white dwarf with a disk-type SED (10,250 K, 0.81 Msun). Madurga Favieres et al. (2024, A&A 688, A168) find no excess in CatWISE (W1 16.49 against a model 16.52).
